# Globex Return Policy

## Return window
Globex allows returns within 60 days of purchase, even if the item has been opened. A restocking fee of 10% applies to opened electronics.

## Refunds
Refunds are issued as store credit by default; customers may request the original payment method within 14 days. The "Globex Flex Credit" program converts store credit into gift balance.

## Exceptions
Custom-made items are non-returnable.
